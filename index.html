Who We Are

Engineering
That Moves

We are Ohlone College's inaugural Formula SAE program — a team of Renegades building something most community colleges never attempt.

Partner With Us →

Formula SAE is a global collegiate design competition organized by SAE International. Teams design, build, and race a small open-wheel race car from scratch, then present it to panels of professional engineers from automotive and aerospace firms including GM, Ford, Honda, and Lockheed Martin.

Being a community college team is not a disadvantage — it is our identity. Our students are working engineers in training, many the first in their families to pursue a technical degree. We bring focus, resourcefulness, and a chip on our shoulder.

We are competing in FSAE Michigan/Lincoln — the largest engineering design competition in the world — alongside 420+ teams from 52 countries. Ohlone Renegades belong on that grid.

The Car

Six Systems. One Machine.

Every subsystem is student-designed. No kit, no shortcut, and no precedent at Ohlone College.

Sponsor a Subsystem →
SYS — 01

Chassis & Structure

4130 chromoly steel space frame, TIG welded by students. Designed to FSAE roll-hoop and impact-attenuator rules with every node calculated before cutting.

SYS — 02

Suspension

Unequal-length double A-arms at all four corners. Custom machined uprights, Öhlins coilovers, and fully adjustable geometry for event-day tuning.

SYS — 03

Powertrain

Honda CBR600F4i engine through a 20mm SAE restrictor. Custom intake plenum, standalone ECU, and a stainless exhaust routed for maximum ground clearance.

SYS — 04

Brakes

Wilwood Dynalite four-piston calipers at all corners, dual-circuit pedal box with adjustable brake bias, and stainless-braided AN lines throughout.

SYS — 05

Aerodynamics

Single-element front and rear wings in fiberglass. Designed using CFD, tested against FSAE dimensional rules, and paired with a flat underbody floor.

SYS — 06

Software & Data

AiM MXS dash with GPS lap timing, four-corner wheel speed logging, Python lap simulation, TunerStudio ECU mapping, and Deutsch DT harnesses.

Build Schedule

The Road to Competition

Phase 1 • Months 1–2

Design & Planning

Rules study, suspension geometry simulation, chassis design, engine selection, procurement of long-lead items, and SAE registration.

Phase 2 • Months 3–5

Chassis Fabrication

Jig build, tube cutting, and TIG welding of the space frame. Simultaneous machining of suspension uprights, hubs, and pedal box fabrication.

Phase 3 • Months 5–8

Assembly & Integration

Engine install, suspension assembly, brake system, wiring harness, fuel system, and first startup. All subsystems verified before rolling.

Phase 4 • Months 8–11

Testing & Development

Shakedown, data-driven tuning, driver development, reliability runs, bodywork/aero fitment, and competition tech checklist completion.

Phase 5 • Months 12–14

Competition & Finale

Transport to FSAE Michigan/Lincoln, technical inspection, static design & cost judging, and dynamic events (Acceleration, Skidpad, Autocross, Endurance).
